## The table

| Market pattern | Current RocketRide behavior | Real gap | Adopt / defer / reject | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Doc-style transcript editing** (Descript: "Delete a word from the transcript, and that word disappears from your audio or video") | We are already transcript-first: select words → cut / mute / bleep, non-destructive ops on the source timeline, original recording immutable. | None on the core idea. | **Adopted (already have it)** | This is our foundation, not a gap. |
| **Restore removed media** (Descript: right-click the edit boundary → *Restore removed media*; *Ignore* strikes text through but keeps it) | `Operation.enabled` is exactly this — struck-through words restore with one toggle. | None. | **Adopted (already have it)** | Reversibility is the price of entry for transcript editing; we pay it. |
| **Word-gap / wordbar timing nudges** (Descript lets you drag word start/end times) | Ops snap to word boundaries in the node's cut planner, but the browser has no direct handle-drag on timing. | Real, small: resizing an edit is coarse and lives only in the transcript. | **Adopt (this phase, as waveform handles)** | Ships as drag-to-resize on the waveform with `snapToWordGap(ms, words, 120)` — the useful half of the wordbar without letting anyone desync words from audio. |
| **AI rough cut you review, not one that is applied** (Descript *Edit for Clarity*: cleans rambling/repetition/off-topic; "removed words or phrases appear as ignored text so you can review or restore them"; adjustable intensity Low/Medium/Heavy; you can discard all edits) | Suggestions today are deterministic heuristics (silence, filler, profanity, low-confidence) in three nested modes — no LLM pass. | Real: no "tighten this episode" pass with reasons. | **Adopt (this phase, review-first)** | We copy the *shape*, not the mechanism: a proposal file `edits/proposals/pNN.json` that never touches `episode-edits.json`, per-item Apply/Reject, Apply-all-safe gated at confidence ≥ 0.7, Discard, and an original-vs-proposed duration line. Intensity maps to our existing natural/balanced/tight. Stock LLM via the existing `director-chat.pipe`; ids only, never timestamps. |
| **AI regenerates speech to smooth an edit** (Descript's clarity edits can include regenerated audio via assigned AI Speakers) | Nothing. Every op is a subtraction from the real recording. | Exists, deliberately. | **Reject** | Synthesising words the guest never said is an editorial and consent problem for a podcast tool, and it needs voice cloning we do not have. Cuts, mutes and bleeps only. |
| **Automatic multicam** (Descript: requires a **multi-track sequence with separate camera tracks**; you open *Camera setup* and **manually assign each audio track to its camera**; styles Automatic vs Show-only-active-speaker; cutaway frequency; caveats — it **overwrites existing scenes**, can pick empty tracks and produce blank scenes, and stopping midway does not undo) | One mixed recording, one camera angle. The visual director's pose-based framing (`solo_follow` / stacked) is *reframing inside one image*, plus fit/fill. | Real and large: we have no second track to cut to. | **Defer (next milestone — see `docs/MULTITRACK_DESIGN.md`)** | The published requirement is separate camera tracks plus a speaker-track mapping. We ingest one file. **Fit/fill and pose-based framing must never be marketed as automatic multicam** — that would be a false claim about a feature whose whole point is switching between real cameras. |
| **Filler-word removal with modes and restoration** (Riverside: *Remove filler words*) | Filler detection exists (`FILLER_WORDS`, English list) and produces suggestions the producer applies as cuts; nothing is auto-applied. | **Multitrack projects** (Adobe/Descript, UNVERIFIED for Adobe) | One mixed recording per episode (`source/<upload>`). | Real and structural. | **Defer — designed, not built** | `docs/MULTITRACK_DESIGN.md`. |

| **Waveform + text editing side by side** (Alitu: click-drag waveform selection, split for ad inserts, and text selection → "Cut This Out" with the waveform updating) | A read-only waveform bar (RMS peaks per 100 ms) under a transcript editor. | Real: the waveform shows but does not edit. | **Adopt (this phase)** | Drag-select a source-ms range bidirectionally synced with the transcript selection; ops render as coloured regions (cut / mute / bleep / shorten) with resize handles, Delete removes, U restores, plus a 2 s-before → 2 s-after audition. Splitting for **ad inserts** is deferred to the sequence/EDL work. |
| **Correcting a misheard word without touching audio** (Descript's wordbar/inline-notes model separates what is written from what is heard) | Transcript text was read-only apart from speaker names. | Real: a misrecognised name was uncorrectable and shipped into the captions. | **Adopt (this phase)** | `edits.corrections: [{word_id, text, original}]` where `word_id = "w" + index` into the immutable `analysis/studio/timeline.json`. **Display and captions only — never timing, never audio.** Corrected words carry a subtle underline dot, hover shows the original, one click restores it. |
| **Non-destructive version history you can open and roll back** | `edits/versions/NNN.json` snapshots were written, but the version chips in the UI were display-only — no open, no preview, no restore. | Real: a "safety net" nobody could actually pull. | **Adopt (this phase)** | `loadVersion` + pure `restoreVersion` producing a **new** revision (`version = current.version + 1`, a `restored vK` note appended); historical files are never rewritten. UI: chip → Open (note, created, ops count, duration) / Restore behind a confirmation. |
| **Never lose an edit to a bad load** (implicit in every vendor's cloud editor) | `loadStudio` used `readJsonOr` everywhere: a transport error was indistinguishable from a missing file, produced `emptyEdits`, and autosave could then overwrite real work. Saves were unserialized. | Real and the most dangerous defect on the list. | **Adopt (this phase)** | `readJsonStrict` distinguishing missing from failed (via `exists()` after a failed read); only *missing* yields defaults; any *failed* file shows a retry banner and blocks initialisation and autosave; a single-flight save queue where rapid changes coalesce into one newest pending save and "saved" means the state that finished saving equals the state that was current when it started. |
| **Playhead, transcript and captions always agree** | The canvas treated every player as source time, while rough preview runs on the **output** timeline and a range preview is output time minus the range start — so highlighting drifted after any cut. | Real. | **Adopt (this phase)** | A `PlaybackClock` (`source` / `rough_preview` / `range_preview`) built from the persisted prepared spec's exact `[[src_s, src_e, out_s]]` map when one matches the revision, else a client approximation flagged `approximate: true`. Transcript and waveform always consume source ms; the canvas converts both ways; a position inside a cut resolves to the next kept boundary. |

## Patterns adopted this phase

1. **One clock, honestly labelled** — source vs rough-preview vs range-preview, with the exact source↔output map from the render spec and an `approximate` flag when only the client's estimate is available.
2. **Fail-closed saves** — missing ≠ failed; a failed load never becomes an empty project, and never autosaves over real edits; one in-flight save, coalesced pending writes, truthful "saved" state.
3. **Version restore that is real** — open, preview, restore-as-a-new-revision; history is append-only (Descript's non-destructive posture, applied to project state instead of media).
4. **Review-only suggestions** — low-confidence findings can be examined and marked, never applied; language-gated cleanup with an explicit "not available for this language"; real Apply-All counts including `skipped_conflict`.
5. **Final-program mastering** — assemble intro + card + body + card + outro first, master the assembled program, measure the delivered file, report `loudness_ok`.
6. **Waveform editing linked to the transcript** — drag-select, coloured op regions, resize with word-gap snapping, audition across a cut (Alitu's two-view editing, with our word grid).
7. **Transcript correction that never moves audio** — text ≠ media, with the original always one click away.
8. **An AI rough cut as a proposal** — reasons and categories per item, applied individually, never written into the edit record by the model (Descript's *Edit for Clarity* review posture, without regenerated speech).

## Explicitly rejected or deferred

| Item | Call | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Automatic multicam / active-speaker camera switching | **Defer** | Requires separate camera tracks and an audio-track→camera mapping we cannot produce from one mixed file. Never describe our fit/fill or pose-based reframing as multicam. |
| Speaker diarization | **Defer** | No AssemblyAI / Deepgram / pyannote key exists in this repo; manual speaker painting stays the supported path. |
| Multitrack ingest, per-track mute/gain/enhance/export | **Defer** | Structural change to ingest and to the render spec — designed in `docs/MULTITRACK_DESIGN.md`, not built here. |
| Scene-level layouts and screen-share priority | **Defer** | Depend on multitrack; a per-scene "layout" over a single mixed camera is only a crop. |
| Reordering sections, sponsor/media inserts | **Defer** | Needs an ordered sequence/EDL, which today's flat op list cannot express. Designed, not built. |
| AI-regenerated speech to patch edits | **Reject** | Editorial/consent problem for podcasts and needs voice cloning. Subtractive edits only. |
| "Studio-quality" one-click enhancement branding | **Reject** | Our reversible NR/HP/compression chain is honest; a learned enhancement model is another vendor and another key. |
| Global filter toggles that retroactively un-apply edits (Alitu style) | **Reject** | Conflicts with per-op restore and makes undo history unexplainable. Modes filter what is *offered*; applied ops are never silently rewritten. |
| New custom nodes or new pipelines for any of the above | **Reject (this phase)** | The brief is hardening: the AI proposal reuses the existing chat pipe; everything else is node-internal or browser-side. |
